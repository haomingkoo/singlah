Privacy

Last updated 26 October 2026

SingLah is run by Koo Haoming, an individual in Singapore. Questions go to privacy@kooexperience.com.

What stays on your device

There is no account and no cookies. Your browser keeps:

  • Songs you save, in your browser's database (singlah-db), so they play offline.
  • Your lyrics view choice, in local storage (singlah-display-mode).
  • If you host a party room, its host key in session storage. It is cleared when you close the tab.
  • A copy of the app's files, so it opens without a connection.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

Searching for songs

Your browser sends what you type straight to LRCLib, a free lyrics library, and loads the lyrics from it. LRCLib sees your IP address and your search. Searches do not pass through any server of ours.

The pitch meter

The pitch meter uses your microphone only after you turn it on and your browser asks. The sound is analysed in your browser and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

Party mode

Creating or joining a room connects you to our party server, hosted by Railway. It holds the room code, the song being played and the playback position, in memory only. A room is deleted after an hour without activity or when the server restarts. To stop abuse, the server keeps your IP address in memory for one minute to count requests.

Hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the site and Cloudflare sits in front of it. Like any website host, they see your IP address when you load a page. Fonts are served from this site, not a font service. Most of these companies are outside Singapore.
