SingLah is a free hobby project by Koo Haoming in Singapore. By using it you agree to these terms.

What SingLah does

SingLah shows synced song lyrics from LRCLib and adds romanisation in your browser. It does not play music. Lyrics belong to their songwriters and publishers; SingLah does not host or sell them.

Accuracy

Lyrics and timings come from LRCLib's community contributors and romanisation is generated automatically, so either can be wrong. SingLah is provided as is, without any warranty.

Fair use of the service

Don't use party mode to send abuse, flood the server or get around its limits. Rooms can be closed at any time.

Availability

SingLah can change or stop at any time. If LRCLib is down, search and new songs won't load, but saved songs still play.
